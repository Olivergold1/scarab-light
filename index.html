About the club

Established in 2000, SCARAB began as a group of swimmers who branched out into cycling and running — hence the name: Swimmers Can Also Run And Bike.

🏅 All abilities welcome

From complete beginners to seasoned Ironman finishers, our sessions are structured so everyone can take part and improve at their own pace.

🤝 Run by volunteers

Our coaches give their time for free, which keeps membership costs low and the club's spirit friendly and community-focused.

📍 Based in Wilmslow

We train at Wilmslow Leisure Centre and on routes across Cheshire and South Manchester, with strong links to our sister club, the Wilmslow Striders.

Training

Structured swim, bike and run sessions through the week, plus member workshops.

Swimming

Coached pool sessions focused on drills and technique. Head coach: Andy.
Wilmslow Leisure Centre · Wednesdays 19:00–20:30.

Cycling

Weekly group rides from social tempo to race pace, with Saturday long rides and midweek intervals, plus turbo sessions over winter. Meet points are shared on the club WhatsApp and Strava.

Running

Run with our sister club, the Wilmslow Striders, who meet outside Wilmslow Leisure Centre every Tuesday and Thursday at 18:30. All abilities welcome.

🧰 Workshops

Bike maintenance, transition practice and race-prep sessions to help members get the most out of race day.

Kit & equipment

What to bring for each session — no specialist gear needed to get started.

Swim

  • Goggles
  • Swim cap (optional)
  • Pull buoy (optional)
  • Kickboard (optional)
  • Fins (optional)

Bike

  • Roadworthy bike
  • Helmet (mandatory)
  • Front & rear lights
  • Spare tube & pump / CO₂
  • Weather-appropriate clothing

Run

  • Running shoes
  • Hi-vis for winter evenings
  • Hydration for longer runs

🎽 Club kit (optional)

SCARAB-branded tri suits, cycling jerseys and running vests. Kit orders open a few times per year.

Membership

Thinking about giving triathlon a go? You're invited to come along to a couple of swim sessions before deciding whether to join.

Benefits
  • Coached swim & run sessions
  • Access to club rides & events
  • Discounts on race entries, such as the Wilmslow Spring Triathlon
  • Members-only WhatsApp group & club Strava

Membership is kept deliberately low-cost because all of our coaches are volunteers. Your fee also covers public liability insurance, retaining essential qualifications (First Aid, coaching) and the equipment used in sessions.

Meet the team

A friendly, qualified bunch who keep the club running and the sessions going.

Simon Clapham

Lead Coach & Founder

Simon founded the club when there was only one other triathlon club in South Manchester, and has been our lead coach ever since. He holds Level 3 Swim and Level 2 Triathlon coaching qualifications and is a multiple Ironman finisher, most recently Ironman Wales in 2018.

Andy Preece

Head Swim Coach — ASA Level 2

Leads our structured pool sessions with a focus on technique, drills and endurance.

Jason Stott

Volunteer

Susan Dickinson

Governance

Our club is run by volunteers and follows clear policies to ensure a safe, inclusive and well-managed environment for all members.

Club policies

We follow guidance from British Triathlon and maintain policies covering:

  • Code of Conduct
  • Health & Safety
  • Data Protection (GDPR)
  • Equality, Diversity & Inclusion

Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of all members is our top priority. We follow British Triathlon safeguarding standards and ensure:

  • All coaches are DBS checked
  • Clear reporting procedures
  • Trained Welfare Officers

Annual General Meetings
